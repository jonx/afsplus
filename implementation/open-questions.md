# Open Questions

Decisions that are deliberately unresolved. No agent settles one of them
implicitly by picking the easiest implementation; each is closed by an ADR
that records the decision, after which its row becomes a link to that ADR.
The plan that produces the deciding evidence is [ROADMAP.md](../ROADMAP.md)
Stage B; the review rules are in [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md).
